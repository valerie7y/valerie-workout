# How Valerie's favorites shaped the app (2026-10-03)

## What her favorites have in common
- Made for people over 40/50: strength for long life, not extreme.
- Short and simple: 15-20 minute follow-along, a few rounds, timed work/rest.
- Kettlebells and bands, plus light dumbbells.
- Balance and fall prevention, mostly from physical therapists.
- Core and back health: planks, holds, posture, back pain.
- Hip and glute work that protects the knees.
- Gentle mobility for hips, spine and shoulders. Walking at home.

## How the app uses this
- Favorite-style moves get picked about 2x as often per move, and nothing was removed.
  In a 100-workout test: ~48% of picks were favorite-style (41% without weighting),
  118 different moves were used, and there were 0 repeats from the last 2 sessions.
- New moves (all knee-safe):
  - Kettlebell: Swing (light), Deadlift (bell raised on a step), Around the World,
    Dead Bug, Overhead March (by a wall), Rack Carry, Floor Pullover.
  - Back and posture: Gentle Superman, Prone Swimmers, Wall Angels, Chin Tucks.
  - Balance (always holding support): Heel-to-Toe + Arm Swings, March + Opposite Arm,
    Rock the Boat, Single-Leg Heel Raise, Single-Leg RDL with a hand on the wall.
  - Mobility: Hip Hurdles (hold wall), Elephant Walk, Lying Hamstring Floss.
- Her own videos as demos:
  - Jenny McClendon's "Ten Best Balance Exercises" is trimmed to the exact part (from the
    video chapters) for Heel Raises, Toe Raises, Heel-to-Toe Stand, Heel-to-Toe + Arm Swings,
    Single-Leg Stand, Heel-to-Toe Walk, March + Opposite Arm and Rock the Boat.
  - Will Harlow's tightrope walk is a second video on Heel-to-Toe Walk (starts at about 2:30; estimated).
  - Tailoredfit shorts are second videos on Elephant Walk, Hamstring Floss and Single-Leg Heel Raise
    (button says which move number to watch).
  - Leslie Sansone "1 Mile Happy Walk": Bonus card on the home screen and second video on Gentle March.

## Skipped and why
- Squats, sit-to-stand, goblet/gorilla squats, lunges, Cossack, skater squat, wall sit: knee.
- Kneeling and hands-and-knees moves (couch stretch, tabletop rotations, 90/90, side plank clamshell): knee.
- Jumping, snatch, clean, jerk, windmill: high impact or too technical.
- Grapevine, side lunge and big step back from the balance video: twisting or lunge-like.
- Facebook reels: used for ideas only, not embedded.
- YouTube DJIn9dNKed0: private or not embeddable.
- JeremyFunctionalFit short and Lebe Stark videos: no chapter times, so they couldn't be trimmed.
  Their style is in the new kettlebell moves.
- nourishmovelove.com plan: blocked by a bot check.
