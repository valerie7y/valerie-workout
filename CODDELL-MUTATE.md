# Coddell Mutate kettlebell core moves (2026-10-04)

Source: his Facebook reels ("20 REPS. 4 MOVEMENTS. 1 KETTLEBELL": uppercuts, underhand twists, sword chops, side-to-side pullovers, diggers; and "swing chops, around the worlds, diggers"), plus his Instagram/Threads captions (halos, renegade rows, pull-throughs). He does them all kneeling. No YouTube channel for him was found, and Facebook is never embedded, so each move uses a checked YouTube demo (see TRIM-LOG.md).

Default is the knee-safe version. Each move also has a "Kneeling version (use knee pads)" switch on the exercise and preview screens. The app remembers the choice per move (localStorage `vw_kneel`) and shows: "Use knee pads or a folded towel. Stop if your knee hurts."

| Move in the app | Default (knee-safe) | Kneeling option demo |
|---|---|---|
| Sword Chop (seated) | seated on the bench, high-to-low chop | tall-kneeling chop and lift |
| Digger (seated low-to-high chop) | seated on the bench | tall-kneeling low-to-high chop |
| Underhand Twist (standing) | standing, feet hip-width, soft knees (or sit) | kneeling twist |
| Side-to-Side Pullover (rainbow) | standing by a wall (or sit) | no checked YouTube demo; links to his reel |
| Kettlebell Halo (existing, now tagged) | standing or seated | tall-kneel halo |
| Kettlebell Around the World (existing, now tagged) | standing | tall-kneel around the world |

Weights: start at 18 lb (8 kg). When it goes up or down, it moves one bell size (9/13/18/22/26/31/35 lb = 4/6/8/10/12/14/16 kg). Reps are 8-12 each side (8-10 for halo and around the world).
Picking: Day D's two core slots give these moves 3x weight (about 46% of core picks in a 400-run test). Days A, B and C now end with a 2-round core finisher that gives them 4x weight (about 55-60% of finisher picks). The finisher doesn't count toward the ~20-minute fit, so it adds about 3 minutes.

Skipped: uppercuts (a fast one-arm punch driven by a hip and back-foot pivot), swing chops (a ballistic swing that needs a pivot), renegade rows and plank pull-throughs (a full plank on hands and toes, with no knee-down option for her), clean + halo (the clean), squat diggers and lunge pass-throughs (squat/lunge), and shoulder-to-overhead, high pull and upright row + press (shoulder moves she already has).
