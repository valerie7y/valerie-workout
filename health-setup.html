Shortcuts could not find the shortcut Log Valerie Workout. Please make it first (steps below), or check the name.

One time only: make a small Shortcut on your iPhone (about 5 minutes).

  1. Open the Shortcuts app. Tap + (top right).
  2. Tap the name at the top. Tap Rename. Type exactly:
    Log Valerie Workout
    Tap Done.
  3. Tap Search Actions. Type Get Dictionary Value. Tap it.
  4. In this action: tap Key, type durationMin.
    Tap Dictionary, choose Shortcut Input.
  5. Search again: Log Workout. Tap it.
    Type: Traditional Strength Training.
    Duration: tap, choose Dictionary Value, unit min.
  6. Tap Done (top right). The Shortcut is ready.
  7. Come back here. Tap Save to Apple Health. Tap Open, then Allow (only the first time).

When it's done, open the Workout app from your Home Screen and tap ❤️ Save to Apple Health again.
